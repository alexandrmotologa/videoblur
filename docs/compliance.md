# Legal Compliance and Regulatory Playbook

This document details how videoblur adheres to federal, state, and international statutory privacy frameworks for video and audio redaction.

## Statutory frameworks

### 1. Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)
Under 5 U.S.C. § 552, public agencies are mandated to release public records while safeguarding statutory exemptions:
- **Exemption 6**: Personnel and medical files whose disclosure would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy.
- **Exemption 7(C)**: Records or information compiled for law enforcement purposes, to the extent that production could reasonably be expected to constitute an unwarranted invasion of personal privacy.

videoblur enforces Exemption 6 and 7(C) by masking all bystander and civilian faces, license plates of non-involved vehicles, computer monitors showing department databases, and audio mentions of civilian identities or phone numbers.

### 2. FBI CJIS Security Policy (v5.9)
The Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS) Security Policy mandates strict handling of digital multimedia evidence (DME) collected by law enforcement:
- **Zero Cloud Transmission**: Unredacted police bodycam, dashcam, and interview room recordings often contain unreleased evidentiary material. videoblur executes entirely offline on local computing hardware. No video or audio bitstreams are transmitted to external APIs or cloud providers.
- **Access Control and Integrity**: Section 5.10 requires verification that digital evidence has not been altered outside authorized redaction procedures. videoblur produces an Ed25519-signed SHA-256 audit manifest documenting every masked interval and frame hash.

### 3. HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR § 164.514)
Under the HIPAA Safe Harbor standard, health systems releasing clinical video (for education, research, or legal responses) must remove 18 specific identifiers, including:
- Full face photographic images and any comparable images
- Names of patients and relatives
- All geographic subdivisions smaller than a state
- Telephone numbers, fax numbers, email addresses
- Social Security numbers, medical record numbers (MRN), account numbers
- Computer screens displaying electronic health record (EHR) workstations

videoblur combines visual monitor detection with spoken PII extraction to ensure comprehensive PHI removal across clinical footage.

### 4. Courtroom Chain of Custody (FRE 902(13) and 902(14))
Under Federal Rules of Evidence 902(13) and 902(14), certified records generated by an electronic process or system that produces an accurate result are self-authenticating if accompanied by a certification of authenticity.

The `redaction_audit.json` manifest generated by videoblur contains:
1. Input and output file path descriptors.
2. Complete counts of visual objects masked by category.
3. Timestamp-aligned audio PII intervals with confidence scores.
4. Cumulative SHA-256 root hash across all redacted video frames.
5. Ed25519 digital signature verifiable with `videoblur certify`.
